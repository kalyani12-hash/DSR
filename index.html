CONSTRUCTION

Specifications and progress.

RCC Structure

RCC framed structure with reinforced shear walls, designed for wind and seismic loads.

Doors & Windows

8-ft veneered doors with quality hardware. Aluminium sliding windows and French doors with mosquito mesh provision.

Flooring

Premium vitrified tiles in living areas and bedrooms. Anti-skid tiles in toilets, balconies and utility. Granite/marble finishes in selected common areas.

CP & Sanitary

Reputed-brand CP and sanitary fittings. Wall-mounted EWC, flush valve and health faucet in all bathrooms.

Electrical

Concealed copper wiring, modular switches and 3-phase supply. AC provision in living and bedrooms with dedicated MCBs.

Telecom & Internet

Intercom to security, DTH provision in living and master bedroom, plus wired internet and telephone point in living.

Generator Backup

100% DG backup with acoustic enclosure and AMF panel for flats and common areas.

Fire & Safety

Fire hydrants, sprinklers, alarms and public-address systems across floors, basements and parking areas.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked.

Where exactly is CIEL by DSR located?

CIEL is in Gopanpally, Hyderabad, Telangana 500075. The Financial District is about 15 minutes away and Neopolis about 20, Wipro's Gopanpally campus about 5 minutes, Glendale Academy about 12 minutes, and Rajiv Gandhi International Airport about 35 minutes via the ORR, at off-peak times.

What size flats are available at CIEL Gopanpally?

There are four 3 BHK layouts: 1,905 sqft, 2,205 sqft, 2,420 sqft and 2,800 sqft. The 2,205 and 1,905 sqft plans are available in all blocks, the 2,800 sqft plan in Blocks A, B, E and F, and the 2,420 sqft plan in Blocks C and D. The 2,800, 2,420 and 2,205 sqft plans each come in East-facing and West-facing versions, and the 1,905 sqft plan is North-facing.

What is the RERA number for CIEL by DSR?

CIEL is registered under Telangana RERA with registration number P02400010929.

When is possession of CIEL by DSR?

Possession is scheduled for 2030. Construction is in progress - the site progress section on this page is updated with dated photographs and a walkthrough film from the latest site visit.

How many flats and towers are there at CIEL?

CIEL has 1,140 flats across 6 towers on 7 acres in Gopanpally. Each tower is 3 basements plus stilt plus 38 floors, and 70 percent of the site is left as open landscape around a central Zen garden.

What amenities does CIEL Gopanpally offer?

There are 67 amenities in total: 32 across the grounds and 35 inside a 60,000 sqft G+6 social clubhouse. These include a rooftop swimming pool and an indoor pool, a gym, yoga and aerobics studios, a spa and steam room, a preview theatre, billiards and table tennis, dining and multipurpose halls, tennis and badminton courts, a Zen garden, an amphitheatre and dedicated kids' zones.

Who is the developer of CIEL?

CIEL is developed by DSR Prime Spaces, part of the DSR Group. DSR has been building in South India for 38 years.

How do I book a site visit to CIEL Gopanpally?

You can book a visit using the form on this page, or call the CIEL Sales and Experience Center on +91 99999 99999. The team can also send you the brochure and current price details.
